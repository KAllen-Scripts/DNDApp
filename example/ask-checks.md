# Ask checks for the example campaign

Questions to put to Ask after feeding in the transcripts (see [transcripts/README.md](transcripts/README.md)),
logged in as the account named. **After** is the first session it can be answered after. Each row says
what a good answer contains, and what must **not** come out for that asker. The "must not" column is the
important one: privacy is enforced on the server, and these are the places a leak would show.

Players: `test-mags` (Wren), `test-theo` (Bran), `test-jules` (Pip), `test-priya` (Oriel). DM: `test-rowan`.
