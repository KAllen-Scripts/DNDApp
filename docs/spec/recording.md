# Recording sessions from Discord (SPEC §6.12)

Owner (2026-10-29): "an integrated bot that joins discord calls and transcribes audio"; "The transcript feed covers all options, so we keep it either way. What we add is an integration with discord"; "We won't take it off server, so no groq or such". Research: `/mnt/project-files/research/discord-transcription/` (project files).

- **The bot** (`src/recording/discord-bot.js`) runs inside the server when `DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN` is set (started by `index.js`, never by the CLI or tests). discord.js with `@discordjs/voice` 0.19.2+ and `@snazzah/davey`, for Discord's end-to-end encrypted calls (DAVE, required since March 2026). Hearing calls was never officially supported by Discord; uploads from anything else stay the fallback.
- **Linking:** each campaign's settings hold `discord: { guild_id, channel_id }` (`normalizeDiscordSettings`), set on the Sessions tab from the servers and voice channels the bot is in. Any DM on the host's server can pick any Discord server the bot is in (they're all the host's group).
- **Starting and stopping:** the Sessions tab's Start/Stop (DM), or `/record` and `/stop` in Discord, allowed for members who can manage the Discord server and for the campaign's DM (their Discord username linked to a DM account in the speaker map). `/record` joins the caller's voice channel (else the linked one); with several campaigns on one Discord server it picks the one linked to that channel. The bot posts that it's recording and its nickname shows it. It stops by itself 5 minutes after the channel empties, after 8 hours, or when disconnected; a server shutdown stops it and keeps what was said.
- **Saving:** each burst of someone's speech (ended by `DISCORD_SILENCE_MS` of quiet) is one Ogg Opus clip with its start on the session clock, pauses inside it kept as silence (`recordings.js` `clip`). Other bots aren't recorded, nor anyone who typed `/norecord` (kept in `data/recordings/excluded.json`, every campaign).
- **Transcribing** (`engines.js`, `transcribe.js`), one recording at a time, outside the jobs queue: `local` (Whisper large-v3-turbo through transformers.js, the default) or `whisper-cpp` (`WHISPER_CPP_BIN`, `WHISPER_CPP_MODEL`); never an online service. Clips under 0.4 s are skipped; Whisper is told the campaign's name, character names and glossary terms; lines Whisper is known to invent in noise ("Thanks for watching!") are dropped; a speaker's pieces from one clip join into one line. Output: `[HH:MM:SS] username: text`, in time order. Interrupted transcriptions (and recordings cut off by a restart) run again on start-up (`recover`).
- **Uploading stays the DM's step:** Use for upload fills the ordinary upload form (transcript, the date played); the DM checks the number and who's who, and uploads. Discord usernames are linked to accounts in the speaker map like any transcript name, once.
- **Storage:** `data/recordings/<campaign slug>/<id>/` (`meta.json`, `clips.jsonl`, `clips/*.ogg`, `transcript.txt`), outside the archive; the DM can delete a recording. An uploaded transcript is archived as always.
- **Later:** transcribing during the session so the transcript is ready at once; playing a line's audio from the Sessions tab.
