# Making a map: the map creator (SPEC §6.6)

- **Making a map** (the map creator, DM, owner's request 2026-10-11; first stage 2026-10-12, second stage 2026-10-13): **New map → Make a map** on the map toolbar opens the creator over the whole page (`web/public/map/creator/creator.js`, with `map/creator/panel.js` for the tools and tabs and `map/creator/start.js` for starting a map and floors of a building). The map is a grid of squares (5 ft each; 4 to 60 across and down; Resize adds or cuts at the right and bottom). Everything is drawn in code (`shared/src/map-art.js` and `map-art-assets.js`, `map-art-farm.js`, `map-art-temple.js`; original work, so no licences), over CC0 photo textures and prop pictures since 2026-10-21 (Map art below), seeded by position so a design draws the same every time; the page's preview and the server's picture use the same code.
  - **Starting a map:** the start screen offers **Blank** (a size and a floor to start with, or nothing; the screen says it's only a start, since any map mixes as many floors as the DM paints with Floor, Fill and Room), **Made for you** (one of 18 kinds the server makes from a seed, `shared/src/map-generate.js` with `map-generate-kit.js` and `map-generate-more.js`: Dungeon, Cave, Forest glade, Village, Tavern, Camp, Crypt, River crossing, Ships at sea, Harbour, Temple, Farm, Mine, Graveyard, Wizard's tower, Castle, Sewer and Dragon's lair; each has its own default size, or the DM's own from 16 × 12 to 60 × 60; the same seed and size always make the same map) and **AI draft** (below). Each is shown first, to use or try again; nothing is saved until the DM saves the map.
  - **Tools** (each with a key): **Pick** (V: click a thing, label, road or wall to change it, drag to move it; drag over the floor to pick several, Shift adds), **Room** (Q: a rectangle floored and walled all round, or only floored with "Walls all round" unticked), **Wall** (W: corner to corner, Shift for half squares; a wall or a low wall or fence), **Door** (D: click a wall for a door a square wide or drag along it; also secret doors, windows and archways, each cut out of the wall, with a frame in a wall style), **Curve** (C: an arc wall, drag end to end then bend), **Round** (O: a round tower, room or pit from its middle out, with a floor inside if wanted), **Floor** (B: a 1 to 5 square brush, round or square, or a rectangle; [ and ] change the size), **Fill** (G: one floor's area up to its walls; Fill all covers the map), **Rub out** (E: things, labels, walls and roads, a long wall a square at a time; or the floor), **Road** (P: freehand, or point by point and double-click), **Label** (T), **Place** (A: whole-size things snap to the grid, Shift places freely, R turns the next one, "each one a little different" varies turn and size), **Scatter** (K: many of one built-in thing over a rectangle, close together to few and far) and **Area** (S: copy, cut, clear or keep a rectangle as a stamp; put it down turned or flipped). Undo and redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z or Ctrl+Y, 100 steps), Ctrl+C, X, V and D, arrow keys nudge, Delete removes, R turns, F flips, zoom, and Space to pan.
  - **Floors** (32, `FLOORS`; 29 photo textures, see Map art): built ones with crisp edges (stone tiles, flagstones, dark slabs, chequered tiles, marble, sandstone, bricks, cobblestones, wooden boards, parquet, ship deck, carpet, iron grating, straw), natural ones that run into their neighbours with uneven edges (grass, dry grass, moss, forest floor, dirt, farmland, gravel, sand, cave floor, rubble, ash, snow, ice, mud) and water (marsh, water, deep water with shallows and foam, lava, which glows). Rubble, ice, mud, marsh, water and deep water are difficult terrain.
  - **Walls** in 8 styles (`WALL_STYLES`: stone, brick, wood, cave rock, palisade, hedge, iron, ruined stone), straight or curved (`arc`: a point the wall passes through). Types: wall, low wall or fence (blocks movement, not sight), door, **secret door** (wall to players until found), **window** (blocks movement, not sight) and **archway** (an open doorway). A wall drawn later over an opening goes round it.
  - **Paths** (9, `PATH_KINDS`, drawn smooth): dirt road, cobbled road, footpath, stepping stones, river and stream (difficult terrain), lava flow (gives light), crack and blood trail; each with its own width.
  - **Built-in assets** (356, `ASSETS`; 58 drawn from pictures, see Map art, in 14 groups shown as folders with a search box that also matches tags): Nature 38 (oaks, pines, willows, palms, bushes, reeds, mushrooms, coral...), Rocks 21 (boulders, stalagmites, rubble, bones, cliff edge, hot spring...), Water 8 (bridges, jetty, waterfall, rapids...), Ships 25 (rowing boat, **Ship's deck** (`hull`: a whole deck whose outline is `hullShape`), sailing ship, longship, mast, sail, cannon, helm...), Camp 27 (tents, wagons, catapult, ballista...), Town 26 (market stall, fountain, forge, roofs, gallows...), Farm 23 (haystack, beehives, windmill, water wheel...), Furniture 50 (tables, beds, bookshelves, fireplace, harpsichord, tapestry...), Tavern 26 (bar counter, kegs, bread oven, stage...), Temple 18 (pews, font, great bell, pipe organ, effigy tomb...), Dungeon 42 (**Round floor** (`floor_round`, a round paved floor for towers), pillars, stairs up and down, spiral stairs, ladder, trapdoor, pits, coffins, cages, portcullis, mine rails...), Traps 11 (bear trap, swinging blade, glyph of warding, gas vent...), Treasure 11 (coins, dragon hoard, mimic...) and Magic 30 (magic circle, portal, crystals, elemental nodes, orrery...). Each has a default size, a shape for blocking (round or its rectangle), a layer (on the floor under walls, standing, or overhead like tree tops and roofs), what it blocks (nothing, movement, or sight and movement), sometimes a colour the DM can change, and 33 give light (campfire, fireplace, candles, chandelier, brazier, wall torch, lantern, forge, glowing fungus, magic circle, portal and others). Picked, a thing can be sized, turned, flipped, tinted, put on another layer, sent to the front or back, made to block something else, give light or not, make the ground under it difficult, and **lead to another map** (`to`, with a name: the building's other floors are listed first).
  - **The DM's own assets:** "Add a picture…" uploads a picture (PNG, WebP, JPEG or GIF, 10 MB; a see-through background looks best) to the campaign's list (`map_assets` table, schema v16; archived as `map_assets/<id>/` like items; DM only); placed keeping its shape, it blocks what the DM chose. Taking one off the list doesn't touch maps already using it. They can be placed but not scattered.
  - **Labels** (up to 100): words on the map in three letterings (`LABEL_FONTS`: Old map, IM Fell English; Carved capitals, Cinzel bold; Plain), with size, colour, turn, italic and an outline. The two fonts are open fonts (SIL OFL, `@fontsource` packages); the server registers them for its picture (`maps/fonts.js`) and serves them at `/fonts/...woff2` so the preview matches.
  - **The look** (`look`, the Look tab): the light (`MOODS`: daylight, dusk, night or gloomy; all but daylight darken and tint the picture with light round lit things), a grid drawn on the picture (none, faint or clear), shadows under walls and things, lights that glow, natural floors that blend (or not), and the colour where there's no floor. **Lights in the picture stop at walls** (`lightAreas` in `map-art.js`): walls, closed and secret doors and things that block sight cut each light's glow, worked out with the same line-of-sight code as the map; windows, archways, low walls and fences don't, a light's own thing doesn't, and a torch on a wall shines from just in front of it. This is only the picture: darkness for line of sight is still set on the map itself.
  - **Stamps** (the Stamps tab): pieces of map put down in one go, turned or flipped. 35 come with the creator (`shared/src/map-stamps.js`, in four groups): Rooms (dining table, bedroom, bar counter, kitchen, library corner, throne dais, smithy, alchemist, shrine, guard post, sitting room, map room, barracks bunks, pantry, wizard's study, chapel), Dungeon (torture chamber, trapped hall, treasure hoard, ritual circle, mine face, knight's tomb), Outdoors (campsite, market stalls, graveyard, pond, stone circle, wagon stop, farmyard, kitchen garden, siege line, nomad camp) and Ships (gun deck, ship's helm, quay cargo). The DM keeps their own with the Area tool or "Keep as stamp" on picked things (a clip: the floor, walls, assets, paths and labels of a rectangle; where things lead is dropped), renames them or takes them off the list (`map_stamps` table, schema v17, at most 200; archived as `map_stamps/<id>/changes.jsonl` and restored, like items; DM only).
  - **AI draft:** the DM describes the map in words and can add a sketch (a photo or drawing), and picks a size or lets the AI choose. The AI (task `design`: the maps model at high effort by default, `MODEL_DESIGN` / `EFFORT_DESIGN`; one structured call, purpose `map:design`; the sketch shrunk to 1568 px) plans it in the creator's own pieces: the ground, areas of floor (rectangles or ovals with natural edges), rooms with their floor, wall style and doors (door, secret door, archway or window on a side), other walls, roads and rivers, things from the asset list, scatters, labels and the look, plus a name and notes for the DM. The server turns the plan into an ordinary design (`maps/design-draft.js`, with the generators' builder). It runs in the background (`POST .../maps/design/draft`, then the page asks every 2 s); a finished draft waits an hour to be opened, at most two at once per person, and it counts against `MAP_AI_PER_HOUR`. Bumped `PIPELINE_VERSION` to 16.
  - **Floors of a building (levels):** on the Map tab of a saved made map, the DM adds a floor above or a cellar below (up to 9 each way: Ground floor, First floor..., Cellar, Lower cellar, Level -3...). Each is its own map, the same size and hidden (`POST .../maps/:mid/levels` `{ n, start: copy | empty, name? }`: starting with this floor's floor and walls, or empty), sharing a building (`level: { group, n }` on the map). Stairs, ladders and trapdoors set to lead to another floor become the map's links (`source: 'creator'`), and a token taking them between floors of the same size arrives at the nearest way back, else the same spot. The floor below can be shown faintly while drawing, to line walls up.
  - **Autosave:** unsaved work is kept in the browser 1.5 s after each change (`dndapp.creator.<campaign>.<map or new>`) and offered back the next time the creator opens for that map; saving forgets it.
  - **The design** (`v: 2`; `{ cols, rows, floor: [a string per row, a FLOORS character per square, '.' for nothing], walls: [{ id, x1, y1, x2, y2, type: wall | low | door | secret | window | arch, style, arc? }] on half squares, assets: [{ id, a: built-in key | 'u:<id>', x, y, w, h, rot, flip, tint, layer, blocks, light, difficult, to, label }], paths: [{ id, kind, w, pts }], labels: [{ id, text, x, y, size, rot, colour, font, italic, halo }], look: { void, grid, shadows, glow, mood, soft } }`, up to 1500 walls, 800 assets, 80 paths of 200 points and 100 labels) is stored on the map (`design`, normalised by `normalizeDesign`) and archived with its changes. Designs saved before the second stage read the same way. Players never get it, and nor does the map's live update: the DM's creator fetches it (`GET .../design`); maps say `designed: true` to the DM.
  - **Saving** (`POST /campaigns/:cid/maps/design` `{ name, design }`): the server draws the picture from the design (`maps/design.js`, on `@napi-rs/canvas` in a worker thread, `maps/design-draw.js`, so the server keeps answering while it draws; WebP made by sharp; 140 px a square for maps up to 30 squares on the long side, fewer for bigger ones so the long side stays at most 4200 px, never under 70: `designCell`), archives it, and makes an ordinary map that starts hidden: no AI reading; the grid exact (the picture's px a square, no offset) and 5 ft squares; walls (curves as pieces sharing a group), low walls, fences and windows (obstacles), doors and secret doors, outlines of things that block (a circle as 8 sides, or the turned rectangle a little inside the picture), lights from things that give light, difficult terrain (difficult floors as rectangles, rivers and streams, things marked difficult) and links from things that lead to another map, all with `source: 'creator'`. Archways give nothing. Doors aren't drawn in the picture (only a threshold): the map draws them open or shut as usual. Uploaded assets are checked to be the campaign's own, and where things lead to another map in the campaign.
  - **Changing it later:** Map settings → **Change in the map creator** (in place of "Read again with the AI") opens the design; **Save changes** (`PUT .../design`) draws a new picture (`image-r2.webp`, `-r3`…; the older ones stay in the archive; the image key changes so browsers fetch it) at the map's own px a square (tokens, fog and the DM's walls are in pixels, so a map made at 70 px before sharper pictures stays at 70, and a map made bigger keeps its squares even past 4200 px) and replaces the walls, links, lights and terrain with `source: 'creator'`. Everything else on the map stays: tokens, fog, walls the DM drew on the map, the AI's or a file's. A creator wall that's the same as before keeps its id, a door stays open or locked, a secret door stays found or not, and a link from the same place to the same map keeps its id. A new name is saved too. If the size changed, what players explored is forgotten (it no longer lines up); other pictures of the map (variants) aren't redrawn.
  - **Export:** any map with a grid (made or imported) downloads as a **Universal VTT file** (`GET .../maps/:mid/export.dd2vtt`, DM; Map settings → Other VTTs, and the creator's Map tab): format 0.3 with the picture showing now as PNG, the grid, walls (`line_of_sight`; low walls and windows left out), doors (`portals`; secret doors as plain doors) and lights (range in squares), for Foundry, Dungeondraft and others (`toUvtt` in `maps/uvtt.js`). Also in Edit map, next to Walls from file (2026-10-16). What the format has no place for goes in a `dndapp` block other VTTs ignore: obstacles, raised areas (heights in feet), stairs and ladders, difficult terrain, plus `secret` and `locked` on portals and exact `bright` and `dim` on lights; reading the file back (as a new map or Walls from file) gives the same map, its raised areas, climbs and terrain stored as `source: 'file'` and replacing an earlier file's.
  - **Map art** (owner, 2026-10-21; option 1 of `/mnt/project-files/research/map-assets/map-assets-research.md`, realistic and all CC0): 29 floors and the 8 wall styles are photo textures (Poly Haven and ambientCG), tiled a little bigger than life, natural floors with a second turned layer and soft patches so the repeat doesn't show; water, deep water and lava stay drawn. 58 props are pictures of Poly Haven 3D models rendered from straight above (tables, chairs, beds, chests, wardrobes, shelves, barrels, crates, pots, statues, ladders, rocks and boulders, stumps, ferns, flowers, ships…), each filling as much of its box as its real size says (`fill`), with a soft shadow made once per picture; the other props stay drawn in code, as does any prop the DM recoloured (so the colour shows). Files in `packages/web/public/art/` (about 1.6 MB, WebP, served at `/art/` with an ETag; `CREDITS.md` lists maker and source), listed in `shared/src/map-art-files.js` and read through `shared/src/map-art-library.js`; the server loads what a design uses, the creator loads it as needed and draws in code until it arrives, and the panel's swatches come from one sheet (`thumbs.webp`). Made by `scripts/art/build.js` from the downloads (kept on the owner's PC in `D:\Code\map-assets`, with their manifest; refuses anything not CC0) with the picks in `scripts/art/picks.js`, and the props rendered by `scripts/art/render-props.js` (three.js in headless Chrome, sun from the top left).
  - **Picture assets** (owner, 2026-10-13; researched: `docs/notes/picture-assets-research.md`): (1) CC0 ground textures kept in the repo: built 2026-10-21 (Map art above); (2), not built, asset packs (Forgotten Adventures, 2-Minute Tabletop, Dungeondraft PNG folders; CC0 packs such as Kenney's) read from a folder on the host's PC, like the books, grouped by sub-folder, never in the repo since their licences don't allow bundling (packed `.dungeondraft_pack` files would need unpacking, not checked). Buildings are large picture assets whose blocking the DM marks or AI walls trace. AI-made pictures ruled out (no image model; a paid service).
  - **Not built yet:** sails on the generated ships (the Sail asset can be placed by hand); a ready-made library of creature and asset pictures; asset packs the DM imports (zips of pictures, or a free-licensed pack with its licence kept); per-asset heights; region maps (no hex grid: owner, 2026-10-18, "No hex grid"); scattering the DM's own pictures. Only tried offline: the AI draft hasn't run with the real AI, and the creator hasn't been used in a real browser since the second stage.
