# Combat on maps (SPEC §6.6)

Spell templates, initiative, attacks and spells from the map.

- **Spell templates** (owner's request, 2026-10-07, from the research on other VTTs): anyone who can see a map places areas of effect: a sphere/radius, a cone (as wide at its end as it is long, the 5e cone), a line (with a width) or a cube (its origin is a corner). Sizes are in the map's unit; without a scale a square counts as 5 ft. The Template dialog offers the player's own spells that have an area, read from the spell's range and description (`spellArea`: "Self (15-foot cone)", "20-foot-radius sphere", "a line 100 feet long and 5 feet wide", "10-foot cube"). On a grid the origin snaps to a square's corner and the direction to 15° steps; a token is caught when the middle of any square it fills is inside (off a grid, its centre) (`tokensInTemplate`). Selecting a template lists who it catches, from the tokens that viewer can see, and lights them up. Whoever placed it, or the DM, moves it (drag), turns it 45° or removes it. Stored on the map (`templates`, up to 50) and archived like everything else. Players only get templates they placed or whose point of origin they can see.
- **Initiative** (owner's request, 2026-10-07): the DM starts a fight on a map (`combat`: `{ round, turn, entries: [{ id, init, mod }] }`, null when there's no fight). Every token joins; NPCs and enemies roll straight away, d20 + their Dexterity modifier read from the stat block (`dexModifier`: "DEX 14 (+2)" or the usual Markdown table; 0 without one). A player rolls once for their own token, d20 + the initiative on their character sheet (their own number if they typed one), or types what they rolled at the table; the DM can roll or type any. A player's roll (the turn order's Roll button, or Initiative on their sheet, 2026-10-09) is a dice roll like any other (`POST /campaigns/:cid/roll` with `initiative: true`): the 3D dice show it, it's shared with whoever their tray says, and its total goes into every fight waiting for one of their tokens; the d20's modifier is kept as the entry's `mod`. Only a roll of one d20 plus a number counts; a token that has rolled keeps its number. Rolls are made by the server. Order: highest first; ties go to the higher modifier, then player characters, then by name; not rolled yet goes last. The DM moves the turn on and back (past the last turn starts the next round), adds and takes out tokens, rolls for every NPC not rolled yet, and ends the fight. A player ends their own turn. Taking out or removing a token on its turn passes the turn on. The turn order panel opens by itself when a fight starts; whose turn it is gets a ring on the map. Each row fits the panel with no sideways scrolling (owner, 2026-10-21): the name gives way (…, in full in its tooltip), then hit points as that viewer gets them (numbers, or Hurt / Bloodied / Down), the initiative and the DM's buttons. The panel is **moved by dragging its header**, inside the map's box; where it was put is kept per browser (as a share of the room each way, so it stays on screen when the window, full screen or a panel beside the map changes the map's size), and a double-click on the header puts it back in its corner (`map/combat.js`). On a phone it stays at the top right. Players see only the tokens they can see in the order; on an unseen token's turn they're told it's someone they can't see (`turn: null`, `turn_unseen`). Archived with the map.
- **Attacks and area spells from the map** (owner, 2026-10-21; research: `docs/notes/map-attacks-research.md`, after Foundry with Midi-QOL, Roll20, Owlbear Rodeo, D&D Beyond Maps and Fantasy Grounds). On by default; Campaign settings → **Attacks from the map** turns it off (§6.10).
  - **Attacking:** pick an enemy and press **Attack** on its bar (or pick your own token and press **Attack** to choose a target after). A small dialog lists what the attacker can do: for a player's token, the sheet's weapons, attacks and damaging spells (`sheetActions` in `shared/src/attacks.js`, with the bonuses the sheet already works out, and a Wild Shape form's attacks); for a creature, the stat block's attacks and saving-throw actions (`creatureActions`, 2014 and 2024 wording). It also offers Normal / Advantage / Disadvantage (picked for you from what the server sees: prone, restrained, blinded, invisible, poisoned, frightened, unconscious, a ranged attack with the target 5 ft away; the reasons are shown), any extra damage on a hit (Sneak Attack is ticked when it applies), two hands for versatile weapons and which spell slot to use. One tap on the action rolls. Players act only for their own tokens and companions; the DM for any token.
  - **The server rolls everything** (`POST /campaigns/:cid/maps/:mid/attack`): d20 + bonus against the target's AC (from the stat block, or the player's sheet), a 20 always hits and doubles the damage dice (2024 and 2014 PHB: roll the damage dice twice), a 1 always misses; a paralyzed or unconscious target hit from 5 ft is a crit. Damage takes the target's resistances, immunities and vulnerabilities into account (stat block, or "resistance to … damage" on the sheet and a Wild Shape form). Hit points go down on the token and, for a player character, on their sheet (temporary hit points first). A concentrating creature rolls its Constitution save (DC 10 or half the damage) and loses concentration on a fail; a player is told the DC.
  - **Area spells:** a spell with an area (a sphere, cone, line or cube in its range) asks the caster to place the template; whoever it catches (`tokensInTemplate`; cones and lines not the caster) makes the saving throw: creatures from their stat block, player characters from their sheet. Full damage on a fail, half on a success for spells that say so. Spells with an attack roll target one creature like a weapon; healing spells heal.
  - **Spell slots:** casting spends a slot of the chosen level (or a pact slot) from the sheet (`shared/src/spell-slots.js`, shared with the sheet's own casting; with no level picked, the lowest level with a slot left); "Use a spell slot" can be unticked (a scroll, a free cast). Undo gives the slot back.
  - **What happened** shows as one card per attack beside the map, for everyone who can see it: who attacked whom with what, the roll, hit or miss, the damage, saves. The **Attack log** button (on the toolbar once there's been one) lists the last 30 (`attacks` on the map). The DM's cards have **Undo** (puts the hit points, concentration and slot back), and while waiting for the DM, **Apply**, **Half** and **Skip** per target.
  - **The DM's choices** (`attacks` in the campaign's settings): **Damage from players' attacks**: applied straight away (default) or waits for the DM's OK; **Show players the AC they rolled against** (off by default: a player sees "hit" or "miss", never a creature's AC).
  - **Privacy (`attackLogFor`):** players get only attacks where they can see the attacker or a target; an attacker they can't see is "someone you can't see"; hidden or fogged targets are left out. A creature's AC, saves and hit points are never sent to players (only the attacker's own roll and the DM's choice to show AC); hit points after the attack are told only for player characters and companions.
  - **Extra damage on a hit** (`shared/src/attack-riders.js`): a list of riders, each saying which sheets have it, which attacks it fits, its dice and when to tick it by itself. Sneak Attack (rogues; finesse or ranged weapons; with advantage, or an ally next to the target and no disadvantage) is the first; other class features add theirs there.
  - **A creature's spells** (owner, 2026-10-29): besides its attacks and save actions, a stat block's Spellcasting or Innate Spellcasting trait (`creatureSpellList`: the save DC, the spell attack bonus (or DC − 8), the caster level for cantrips, and each listed spell with how often: "3rd level (3 slots)", "2/day each", a 2024 "Fireball (level 4 version)" cast at 4th) gives one action per spell (`creatureSpells`, `spell:npc<n>`), looked up by name like the sheet's (the SRD, then the books, then the AI's memory; one not found is left out until the server restarts). They work like a character's spells (areas, saves, conditions, concentration, spells in play) with the creature's DC and bonus; no slots are kept (the picker shows its uses for the DM to count) and a higher slot can be picked.
  - **The DM's own** (owner, 2026-10-29): **Effect** on the map bar, or **Something else…** at the bottom of any token's picker, opens a form: a name, who does it (any token, or nobody: a trap, the room, named as the DM likes, "The DM" by default), what it is (an attack roll with a to-hit, a saving throw with its DC, ability and half on a save, damage that just lands, healing, or only an effect), damage ("2d6 fire + 1d4 cold", `customDamage`), conditions it gives (on a hit, a failed save, or straight away), an area (sphere, cone, line, cube and its size, placed like a spell's) or tapped targets (as many as the DM likes), and whether it stays in play (rounds, 0 until ended; concentration when a token does it; difficult terrain in its area). `POST …/attack { custom, attacker?, target | targets | template }` (DM only; `CUSTOM` in routes/attacks.js, `customAction` in shared/attacks.js); nothing is spent and no riders are added. Its card, Undo, Spells in play and Roll for who's in it work as for any spell (a lasting one keeps what the DM filled in as `custom` on the spell, never sent to players). Players see a nobody's effect by its name, only when it touched someone they can see.
  - Not yet: Multiattack in one go, spells with several darts or beams (Magic Missile, Scorching Ray), cover, once-a-turn limits on riders, attacks in the roll log and in map events for the archivist, keeping count of a creature's spell slots or uses a day.
- **Spells that aren't attacks** (owner, 2026-10-28; research: `docs/notes/map-spells-research.md`, after Foundry with Midi-QOL, DAE and its regions, Fantasy Grounds' effects, Roll20, D&D Beyond Maps and Owlbear). Part of Attacks from the map (the same switch).
  - **Every spell on the sheet** is in the picker (`sheetActions`), with what it does in a few words ("DC 13 Str save · restrains · difficult terrain · 20 ft cube · concentration, 1 minute"). A spell with nothing to roll is kind `effect` (Bless, Mage Armor, Invisibility). One with an area places its template, as before ("20-foot square" is a cube's footprint); one with range Self is cast on the caster; a save or effect spell that can take several ("up to three creatures", plus "one additional creature for each slot level above") or none is cast by tapping them (they light up), **On yourself**, then **Cast** (an effect can be cast on nobody); a single-target spell goes on the first tap. The server refuses a player more targets than the spell takes (`targets_max`); the DM can pick any.
  - **Conditions from the spell's text** (`spellConditions` in `shared/src/spell-effects.js`): "or be restrained", "or be paralyzed", "becomes charmed… is incapacitated", "falls prone", 2024's "has the Restrained condition", "becomes invisible"; not ones it can't give ("can't be charmed") or only mentions ("a creature restrained by the plants"); of "blinded or deafened" the first. They go on the tokens that fail the save, or on everyone it's cast on for an `effect`. A stat block's save action gives its conditions too. Undo takes them off.
  - **Spells in play** (`spells` on the map, at most 40, `normalizeMapSpells`): a spell that isn't instantaneous (`spellLasting`: rounds from its duration, a minute is 10, an hour 600; "until dispelled" has none) and has an area or caught someone stays in play: `{ id, name, caster, caster_name, user_id, action, slot, concentration, rounds, ends, template, save, dc, check, affected: [{ id, conditions }], attack, at }`. Its template is marked as the spell's (`spell`), and as `difficult` when the spell says its area is difficult terrain (`makesDifficult`: Entangle, Web, Spike Growth, Grease), which movement then counts like drawn terrain (`isDifficult`). A concentration spell gives its caster **Concentrating** and ends the caster's earlier concentration spell (the card says so).
  - **Ending** (`endMapSpell`): its area goes, the conditions it gave come off (unless another spell in play gives the same one to the same token), and its caster stops concentrating. A spell ends when whoever cast it (or the DM) presses **End spell**, the DM undoes its card, its area is removed, its caster fails a concentration save, casts another concentration spell or has Concentrating taken off their token, or, in a fight, when its time is up (`expireMapSpells`, on each next turn: cast in round R lasting N rounds, it ends as its caster's turn starts in round R + N; `ends: { round, turn }`, set only when cast in a fight). Outside a fight nothing counts the time.
  - **Rolling again** (`POST …/spells/:sid`): **Break free** (Entangle's Strength check with the creature's action, `check`, read from "make a Strength check against your spell save DC") or **Save again** (Hold Person) for one creature under it, by the DM or whoever controls that creature (`rollToEnd`: a success takes its conditions from the spell off; a spell with no area and nobody left under it ends); and **Roll for who's in it**, by the caster or the DM, which runs the spell again (no slot) for whoever is in its area now: one that holds people (Entangle) skips those it holds, one that hurts (Spirit Guardians, Moonbeam) rolls for all. Both go in the Attack log as cards.
  - **Where it shows:** the Attack log opens with **Spells in play** (each with its caster, rounds left, who's under it and with what, and the buttons each viewer may use); picking a spell's area shows the same on the template's bar; a token's bar says "Under Entangle (restrained)"; cards say what each target got and how long the spell lasts.
  - **Privacy (`mapSpellsFor`):** players get spells they cast, or whose caster, someone under it or area they can see; an unseen caster is "someone you can't see"; only the tokens under it they can see; the save DC only of their own.
  - Not yet: buffs that change numbers by themselves (Bless's d4, Shield's +5 AC: the table adds them), auras that move with their caster (move Spirit Guardians' area by hand), rolling when someone enters an area (press Roll for who's in it), and durations outside a fight.
