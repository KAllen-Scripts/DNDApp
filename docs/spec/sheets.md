# Character sheets (SPEC §6.4)

Owner's requirements (2026-10-06): structured like a normal 5e sheet; autofill what can be worked out from the core stats; **anything the player changes by hand must be respected and preserved**; saved between sessions; players can upload an existing sheet; a spells section with full spell details, which can be pulled in by name.

- **Several characters** (owner, 2026-10-21). A player can have up to 20 characters per campaign, each a whole sheet of its own (`sheets/store.js`); one is **active**, the one they play. Everything that reads "the player's sheet" uses the active one, because it all goes through `sheets.get(campaign, player)`: the Sheet and Inventory tabs, their map token (its hit points, speed, height and picture; its name shows the active character's name), rests and hit dice, merchants' purchases, potions, class features, Wild Shape, the DM's Items tab, Review sheet, upload and download. Pictures belong to a character (`character_pictures.number`), so switching changes the token picture too.
  - **On the Sheet tab** (`web/public/sheet/characters.js`), above the sheet: a strip saying **Playing <name> · <race and classes>** and a **Characters (n)** button that opens the list (remembered per browser): each character with **Playing now** or **Make active**, **Rename**, and **Delete** (asked first; not for the one played: make another active first), and **+ New character** (a name, then a blank sheet with the player's name filled in, made active; the one played before is kept). A player with nothing saved yet has their blank first character; a new one then becomes character 1.
  - **Switching is the player's alone** (Claude's call): the DM doesn't see the list or change it, and sees only the active character (on the Items tab, on maps, and by name wherever players are named). Inactive characters stay private to their player: only `GET /characters` (the player's own) lists them.
  - **Who the player plays**: making a character active (or renaming the active one) sets the membership's character name (`auth.setCharacterName`, archived in `members.json`), so speaker names, Ask ("who plays …"), rolls and the archivist's roster follow; the admin can still type it.
  - **Two devices**: the page sends the character's number with each save (`PUT /sheet { character }`); if another character became active elsewhere, nothing is saved over it (409 `switched`) and the page shows the active one. Switches are sent live (the `sheet` event carries `number`) and between windows.
  - **Deleting** only hides a character (`deleted_at`); its archive files stay, and restore keeps it hidden. The archivist reads every character's changes, each marked as the one played now, not played now, or deleted, and is told each switch and deletion with its time (`<characters_changed>`, `kb/updates.js`).
- **Contents** (Sheet tab, `web/public/sheet/sheet.js`): page 1 (name, with a one-line summary such as "High Elf · Wizard 5 / Fighter 1 · Level 6"; class & level with multiclass rows, background, race, alignment, player name, XP, character level; ability scores; inspiration, proficiency bonus, saves, skills marked ○/●/◆ for none/proficient/expertise, passive Perception, other proficiencies; AC, initiative, speed, HP, hit dice (a row per die size with a tick per spent die, Spend and Short rest: §6.8), death saves; attacks; coins and equipment; personality, ideals, bonds, flaws; features); page 2 (details, appearance, allies, backstory, treasure); page 3 (spellcasting class and ability, save DC, attack bonus, slots per level and Pact Magic, each with a circle to tick per slot used; spells grouped by level with prepared ticks). Download (JSON), upload, print.
- **Attacks and spells that roll** (owner's request, 2026-10-09). Worked out in `shared/src/rolls.js`, the same file for the page and the tests.
  - **An attack** (`normalizeAttack` in `sheet.js`) has a name, damage with its type ("1d8 slashing"), and under it: attack roll or saving throw; the ability it adds (Strength, Dexterity, …, Finesse = the better of Strength and Dexterity, Spellcasting ability, or As written = nothing); proficient or not (saved, on by default; since the Inventory there's no tick for it on the page, see below); a magic bonus. To hit = proficiency (if proficient) + the ability's modifier + magic; damage = the dice + the ability's modifier + magic. A saving-throw attack has no roll to hit: its DC is 8 + proficiency + the ability's modifier, against the save ability chosen, and its damage adds only the magic bonus. A number the player types in the to-hit or DC box is kept (blank = worked out; the worked-out one shows in the empty box), like every other automatic value. Typing one of the PHB weapons (`WEAPONS`, SRD) the character carries as a new attack's name fills in its damage and ability; "Unarmed strike" fills in 1 bludgeoning and Strength. The name box suggests "Unarmed strike" and the weapons in the inventory. Attacks saved before 2026-10-09 (and uploaded sheets) have no ability (As written), so their typed bonus and damage roll exactly as before. Under each attack the sheet shows what it will roll ("1d20+5 to hit · 1d6+3 piercing"). Damage with no dice (an unarmed strike's 1) is shown as a number, not rolled.
  - **Equipped weapons** (owner's request, 2026-10-09): each weapon equipped in the Inventory tab (below) shows at the top of Attacks, worked out from the inventory line (it can't be edited there): name (×2 for two daggers), to hit, damage, a d20 to roll to hit (damage offered after, doubled on a natural 20), Dmg, and for versatile weapons 2H (one die size bigger: `gearRolls`). Proficiency comes from the inventory line, so typed attacks no longer have a Proficient tick (their proficiency is still saved and counts; attacks typed by hand are for anything that isn't an item: unarmed strikes, a breath weapon).
  - **Weapon attacks need the weapon** (owner, 2026-10-10). A typed attack whose name names one of the PHB weapons (`attackWeapon` in `gear.js`: "Longsword", "+1 longsword", "Thrown daggers", "Shillelagh (quarterstaff)") needs an inventory line naming that weapon (`ownsWeapon`: "Dagger", "+1 Longsword", "Dagger of Venom"); unarmed strikes, spells, features and natural weapons don't. Without it, the row is marked, its roll buttons are off, and it says so with **Add a Longsword to my Inventory** (adds it from the tables, equipped, with Proficient from the class and the attack's magic bonus, and removes the typed row, since equipped weapons show in Attacks by themselves). The server refuses a save (400, naming the weapons) that adds such an attack or renames one to such a weapon (`newUnownedWeaponAttacks`, against the saved sheet); the page doesn't send it and says "Not saved: you don't have a Longsword in your Inventory…" until it's fixed. Attacks the saved sheet already had (sheets from before, uploaded sheets, a weapon later sold or dropped) stay and save, but don't roll until the weapon is back. Only PHB weapon names are recognised: a homebrew weapon's name ("Sunblade", "My sword") isn't checked.
  - **A spell** has: Roll (none, spell attack, or saving throw with its ability), damage or healing ("8d6 fire", "1d8 healing"), more dice per slot level above its own (for a cantrip: at 5th, 11th and 17th character level), and + spellcasting modifier. These are read from the spell's description when it's added or looked up, or for a spell saved before (`guessSpellRolls`: "make a ranged spell attack", "a Dexterity saving throw", "8d6 fire damage", "regains hit points equal to 1d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier", "increases by 1d6"), and the player can change them in the spell's details. In the spell's row: a d20 to roll the spell attack (spell attack bonus; damage offered after, doubled on a natural 20) or the DC for a save (spell save DC and ability), a slot level to cast it with (1st level and up: each level with how many slots are left, pact slots counted; the lowest with one left to start), and Dmg (or Heal); a spell with nothing to roll has **Cast**. Casting a spell of 1st level or higher spends the slot (owner, 2026-10-28): the to-hit roll for a spell attack, the damage or healing roll for anything else, or Cast. The server spends it (`POST /campaigns/:cid/sheet/spells/cast`, `castWithSlot` in `shared/src/spell-slots.js`, the same rules as casting on the map); with none left at that level the page asks whether to roll anyway (a scroll, an item, a free casting). Cantrips spend nothing. Not handled: more darts or rays when cast higher (Magic Missile, Scorching Ray) and Eldritch Blast's extra beams; the player types those dice in.
- **Inventory** (the players' Inventory tab, `inventory` on the sheet, rules in `shared/src/gear.js`; owner's request, 2026-10-09). Each line: name, kind (as the DM's items), how many, how many equipped, Proficient, needs attunement and Attuned, magic bonus (−5 to +5), weapon stats (damage with type, Strength / Dexterity / Finesse, simple or martial, properties) or armour stats (base AC and light / medium / heavy / shield), weight, description and where it came from. At most 200 lines.
  - **Adding** (tidied 2026-10-21, owner: "This should be cleaner… It always suggests weapons regardless of what category I select. And I feel like there should be some way to check an items stats before you add it"): one row, **Kind** (Any kind, Weapon, Armour, Gear, Tool, Potion, Scroll, Magic item, Other), the name, **Look up**, **Add by hand**. The kind decides the names suggested while typing (`itemNames` in `shared/src/item-names.js`: the PHB's weapons, armour, adventuring gear, tools and potions, spell scrolls and the SRD's magic items; Any kind suggests them all) and the kind Add by hand makes. Look up (or picking a suggestion) shows the item's **card** under the form before anything is added (`items/item-card.js`): its kind, rarity, attunement, a weapon's damage and properties, armour's AC, type, Strength and Stealth, charges, what it heals, what it does to the sheet, weight, where it came from and its description rendered from Markdown, with **Add** and **Cancel**; the inventory line keeps the rarity and shows it. The lookup: the 37 PHB weapons and 13 armours (`WEAPONS`, `ARMOR`, SRD) come straight from the tables with no AI call, also with a magic bonus ("+1 Longsword", "Longsword +1"); anything else is looked up like the DM's items (§6.9: the group's books, then the AI's memory, counted in `SHEET_AI_PER_HOUR`) but never in the DM's own items (`GET /campaigns/:cid/gear/lookup?name=`; nothing is saved on the server until the sheet is). A weapon or armour not in the tables gets its stats read from the description (`itemStats`: "1d8 slashing", finesse, versatile, "AC 14", heavy). **Add by hand** adds a line of the kind picked beside it (owner, 2026-10-16) and opens its Details with that kind's fields: a weapon's damage, ability and magic bonus, armour's type, base AC (or a shield's bonus) and magic bonus, what a potion heals. Changing a line's kind swaps the fields (and keeps the details open). Items bought from a merchant are added the same way (§6.9), keep the item's id (`item_id`) and show its picture, if the DM gave it one.
  - **Sorted by kind** (owner, 2026-10-10): the tab shows the lines under headings in a fixed order, Weapons, Armour and shields, Magic items, Potions, Scrolls, Adventuring gear, Tools, Other, each with how many items it holds, by name within each (a "+1" in front doesn't count; `inventoryGroups` in `gear.js`). A line with weapon stats is a weapon and one with armour stats is armour, whatever its kind; changing a line's kind moves it. Only the display is sorted: the saved order doesn't change.
  - **Proficient** is ticked when a line is added if the character's first class is proficient with it (`CLASS_GEAR`, PHB: weapon categories or named weapons, armour types); unknown classes and non-weapons get it ticked. The player can change it; it's never changed for them again.
  - **Equipping:** weapons: as many as you carry (a number box when you have more than one: two daggers). Armour: one body armour and one shield at a time; equipping another takes the first off, with a message saying so (`normalizeInventory`, which the server applies too). Anything else can be marked equipped (a ring, a cloak) so the DM sees it.
  - **AC** is worked out from what's equipped (`armorClass`): light armour + Dex, medium + Dex up to 2, heavy flat, + shield, + magic bonuses; with nothing worn, 10 + Dex (+ shield). Unarmored Defense still applies when better: a monk's with no armour or shield, a barbarian's with no armour (a shield adds). A typed AC still wins.
  - **Weight** (owner, 2026-10-09): each line has a weight (each; PHB weights come from the tables, the DM's items and the AI give theirs), and coins weigh 1 lb per 50. The Inventory tab shows what's carried against the campaign's weight rule (§6.10, `encumbrance` in `gear.js`): **carrying capacity** (default; Str × 15 lb, doubled for Powerful Build races; over it, speed 5 ft), **variant encumbrance** (over Str × 5: speed −10 ft; over Str × 10: speed −20 ft and disadvantage on Strength, Dexterity and Constitution checks, saves and attacks; over Str × 15: speed 5 ft) or **ignore weight limits** (weights are still added up). The Strength used is the one magic items give.
  - **Heavy armour:** the tables carry each armour's Strength requirement and Stealth disadvantage; without the Strength, speed −10 ft; in noisy armour, Stealth rolls have disadvantage. Both are shown under the sheet's Speed and on the Inventory tab.
  - **Disadvantage from the sheet** (`rollDisadvantage`): rolls the sheet says have disadvantage (Stealth in noisy armour; Strength, Dexterity and Constitution checks, saves, initiative and attacks when heavily encumbered) are sent with disadvantage, or normally if they also had advantage (Shift or Next d20), labelled with why ("Stealth (disadvantage: Chain mail)").
  - **Potions** (owner, 2026-10-16): a line can heal when drunk (`heal`: dice or a number; the PHB's four healing potions by name, `POTION_HEALING`, else read from the description, "You regain 2d4 + 2 hit points"; or typed under Details for a potion). The row has **Drink**: the server rolls it as a shared roll (`POST /campaigns/:cid/sheet/gear/drink { id }`, `rests.drink`), adds the hit points up to the maximum and takes one away (the line goes at none; `drinkPotion` in `gear.js`), and saves the sheet with the reason ("drank Potion of Healing (rolled 7, regained 7 hit points)").
  - **Charges:** a line can have charges (read from the description: "has 7 charges… regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn"; or typed under Details). The row shows how many are left with **Use 1** and **Recharge**: dice are rolled as a shared roll and that many come back; a number or "all" comes back without a roll. Nothing recharges by itself, and the DM's long rest doesn't touch them (owner, 2026-10-10).
  - **Item actions** (owner, 2026-10-21: items that give abilities): a line's `actions` (`shared/src/item-actions.js`, at most 8) say what it lets its owner do: `summon` (a creature from a table rolled on the server, or a set one, with a count that can be dice), `spell` (with its level, save DC and ability, damage and the dice each extra charge adds), `attack`, `heal` or `other`; each with `cost` (charges), `consumes` (one of the item is used up: a bead, a gem, a card), `needs` (equipped or attuned), `until`/`expires` for summons and `source`: *rules* (items the app knows by name, per edition, from the DMG 2014 and 2024: Bag of Tricks gray/rust/tan, the Figurines of Wondrous Power, Horn of Valhalla, the elemental summoners, Elemental Gem, Cube of Summoning (2024), Deck of Illusions, Necklace and Wand of Fireballs, Pipes of Haunting, Ring of Djinni Summoning, Staff of the Python; `rule` names the entry so the server works it out again for the campaign's edition), *text* (read from the description's usual wording: "expend 1 of its charges to cast the X spell (save DC N)"), *ai* (the item-reading AI lists them on lookup, marked "AI: check it" on the sheet) or *manual*. The rules the app knows win over the AI's; the AI's win over nothing. The AI's `uses` fills in the charges when the description doesn't say.
  - **The Item actions box** (`web/public/sheet/item-actions.js`, under Attacks in every layout; hidden when nothing has actions) lists each item's actions with its charges left, a summary, what stops it ("Attune to … first", "0 charges left (they come back at dawn)") and **Use** (plus "Extra charges" for Fireball). The item's card (Adding) and its Details list them too. Add by hand fills in the rules for an item the app knows.
  - **Use** (`POST /campaigns/:cid/sheet/gear/use { id, action, extra?, visibility? }`, players): the server checks it can be used, spends the charges (and the item, if it's used up), rolls and does it, saves the sheet with the reason and answers like a roll plus `{ sheet, version, rolls, summoned, placed, crumbled, healed, damage, message }`. A **summon** rolls the table (Bag of Tricks' d8) and how many, takes the creature's stat block from the item (the Ebony Fly's giant fly) or looks it up like a Wild Shape form (SRD, books, AI; a bare one to fill in if nobody knows it), adds each as a companion (kind summon, from the item, `vanishes`: gone at 0 hit points) and puts them on the map next to the character if they're on one. A **spell** rolls its damage (Fireball: 8d6, +1d6 for each extra charge or bead, up to the item's limit; 2024 Wand of Fireballs at most 3 charges), with the DC in the roll's label. An **attack** rolls to hit and offers the damage. A **heal** adds the hit points. A wand that crumbles rolls a d20 when its last charge goes: on a 1 it's gone. Potions keep their own **Drink**.
  - **Summons that vanish:** a companion with `vanishes` goes from the sheet (and so from the map) when its hit points reach 0, on the sheet or its token. One with `expires: 'dawn'` (2024 Bag of Tricks) goes when the DM's campaign clock passes 6 in the morning (§6.6 clock), noted for the DM. Charges still never come back by themselves (owner, 2026-10-10).
  - **Attunement:** at most three attuned items (an artificer four at 10th level, five at 14th, six at 18th; `attunementLimit`); ticking a fourth is refused with a message, and the server keeps only that many. Only items that need attunement can be attuned. The Inventory tab shows "Attuned to 2 of 3".
  - **Magic item effects** (`itemEffects`, `activeEffects`): a line can change the sheet while it's equipped (and attuned, if it needs attunement): +AC; +AC with no armour or shield (Bracers of Defense); + all saves or one ability's saves; an ability score set to at least a number (Gauntlets of Ogre Power: Strength 19; the sheet shows "19 with items" under the score) or raised; speed; initiative; spell attack and spell save DC. Read from the description's usual wording ("+1 bonus to AC and saving throws", "Your Strength score is 19", "+2 bonus to spell attack rolls and to the saving throw DCs", "walking speed increases by 10 feet"), and listed and editable under Details (at most 8). Everything else a magic item does (spells, resistances, flying) stays in its description. Typed values still win.
  - The free-text **Equipment** box stays for anything else the player wants to write.
- **A compact sheet** (owner, 2026-10-28). Options open when asked (`web/public/sheet/fold.js`; a fold remembers being opened while the page is open, so redraws don't close it): the Wild Shape forms and Add form (**Turn into a form (n)**; open while there are none), adding a companion, adding a spell (open while there are none), and spell slot levels the character has no slots in (**Show all levels**, to type a number into one). A class feature's text shows as one line (its worked-out numbers come first) until its name or the line is clicked. Nothing is taken away: it's the same controls, folded.
- **Wild Shape** (owner, 2026-10-14). Rules in `shared/src/wild-shape.js`, the page in `web/public/sheet/wild-shape.js`, kept on the sheet as `wild_shape: { forms, active }`; its uses are the Wild Shape row of Class features (below), `class_features.spent.wild_shape` (sheets saved before then had `wild_shape.uses_spent`, moved over when read).
  - **Shown only on a sheet with a druid class**, under the Wild Shape row of the Class features box. It follows the campaign's rules edition (§6.10; the page gets it with the sheet), unlike the rest of the sheet's numbers.
  - **Forms** are added by name (the SRD's 87 beasts and four elementals are suggested while typing): `GET /forms/lookup` gives the SRD's stat block at once (`sheets/srd-beasts.json`, no AI), else the group's books (the AI copies the printed stat block), else the AI's memory, labelled as such and counted in `SHEET_AI_PER_HOUR`; never the DM's own creatures. A form keeps size, type, CR, AC, hit points, speeds, the six scores, listed saves and skills, senses, traits and actions (to hit, save DC, damage, text). Each form shows **Turn into**, or why not yet.
  - **Limits by druid level** (`wildShapeRules`): **2014**: from level 2, two uses per short or long rest (unlimited at 20); Beast Shapes table: CR 1/4 with no flying or swimming speed, CR 1/2 with no flying from 4, CR 1 from 8; Circle of the Moon CR 1 from 2, druid level ÷ 3 from 6, elementals from 10 for two uses. **2024**: two uses, three from 6, four from 17; a short rest gives one back, a long rest all; same CR limits, flying from 8, swimming any time; forms known 4, 6 from 4, 8 from 8 (a note when there are more on the sheet, not enforced); Circle of the Moon CR up to level ÷ 3 (at least 1). Uses are ticks like spell slots, and the rests (§6.8) give them back.
  - **Turning into a form** spends a use and shows the form above the list and on a line under the character's name. **2014**: the form has its own hit points (full to start), typed on the sheet or changed from the token on the map (§6.6: the token shows the form's hit points and speed while it lasts); at 0 or below the druid changes back and the damage left over comes off their own hit points (`setFormHp`), saved with the reason. **2024**: the druid keeps their own hit points and gains temporary hit points equal to their druid level (three times for Moon); a Moon druid's AC is at least 13 + Wisdom modifier.
  - **In a form** (`formStats`): the beast's Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, AC, speeds and actions; the druid's Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma; saves and skills use the druid's proficiencies with the new scores, or the beast's listed bonus when higher. Ability checks, saves, the proficient skills, initiative and each attack (to hit, then damage) roll from the box. **Change back** ends it. The spells on the sheet are never locked in a form (owner, 2026-10-14); the box only says that casting needs druid level 18 (Beast Spells), and from 18 that they can. Not built: Combat Wild Shape's healing (left for later work on subclasses), keeping track of the hours.
- **Companions** (owner, 2026-10-21). Rules in `shared/src/companions.js`, the page in `web/public/sheet/companions.js` (a **Companions** box under Class features in every layout), kept on the sheet as `companions: [{ id, name, kind (familiar, pet, mount, summon, sidekick, animal companion), form (a stat block shaped like a Wild Shape form), hp: { current, max }, from: { kind (spell, feature, item), name, item_id }, until, expires ('' or 'dawn'), notes }]`, at most 20 (`MAX_SHEET_COMPANIONS`).
  - **Adding:** "A creature by name" looks the stat block up like a Wild Shape form (the SRD's creatures, then the group's books, then the AI's memory, marked; never the DM's creatures), or a spell or feature the sheet has (`companionSources`): **Find Familiar** (the edition's list of forms; also a Pact of the Chain warlock), **Wild Companion** (druid 2+, lasts half the druid level in hours), **Find Steed** (2014: warhorse, pony, camel, elk, mastiff; 2024, and every 2024 paladin from level 5: the Otherworldly Steed worked out for the slot level and Celestial, Fey or Fiend), **Find Greater Steed**, **Conjure Animals** (2014: type the beasts; 2024: the spirit pack with its Dexterity save and damage for the slot), **Primal Companion** (Beast Master: Beast of the Land, Sea or Sky; Tasha's for 2014, the PHB's for 2024), the 2014 **Ranger's Companion** (the beast with the proficiency bonus added to AC, attacks, damage, saves and skills, and at least four times the ranger level in hit points), the **Drakewarden's drake** (Fizban's, by level and essence) and the **Battle Smith's Steel Defender** (Tasha's). The worked-out ones (`builtStatBlock`) use the sheet's proficiency bonus, spell attack, spell save DC and level; no AI call. A name and kind can be given.
  - **Each companion** shows its hit points (typed; at most its maximum), AC, speed, size and type, where it came from and how long it lasts, its attacks (d20 to hit with damage after, Dmg, Heal), and under **Stat block** its ability checks and saves to roll, senses, traits and source.
  - **Put on the map** (`POST /campaigns/:cid/sheet/companions/:id/map { map_id? }`, default the map the character is on) makes a companion token tied to the entry (`companion` on the token): next to the character and following them, on one map at a time (a token of it elsewhere goes), so it travels with them. The token's name, stat block and hit points are the sheet's (`maps.view`), and a change to its hit points on the map goes on the sheet (`maps.setCompanionHp`), like a character's token. **Take off the map** (`DELETE …/map`) leaves it on the sheet; **Dismiss** takes it off the sheet and any map (the server removes tokens of companions no longer on the sheet when it's saved). `GET /campaigns/:cid/sheet/companions/where` says which map each is on. The map's **Companion** button (now one of the main map tools, so it shows in full screen) lists the sheet's companions first, with Put on the map, then the old form for one that's only for that map.
  - Privacy as for any companion token (§6.6): only its player controls it and gets its stat block; everyone who sees it sees its hit points.
- **Class features** (owner, 2026-10-14; design agreed in the thread: one list of rules and one box, Wild Shape part of it, the DM sees it, Rage as charges like spell slots, rows of the player's own). Rules in `shared/src/class-features.js`, the page in `web/public/sheet/class-features.js`, kept on the sheet as `class_features: { spent: { id: n }, max: { id: n }, custom: [rows], rolled: { id: [d20s] } }`.
  - **One box, in every layout under Attacks** (in Tabs, on the Actions tab), showing the rows this character's classes give at their level, for the campaign's rules edition (each row uses its own class's level, so a multiclass character sees both classes' rows, named with the class). Every sheet has it, so anyone can add their own rows.
  - **Kinds of row:** *uses* (tick boxes like spell slots, or a number with − and + past 10), *pool* (points spent from a number: Ki/Focus Points, Lay on Hands, Sorcery Points) and *info* (a worked-out number, nothing to spend: Sneak Attack dice, Martial Arts die, Aura of Protection, Relentless Rage, Infusions). Maximums are worked out every time from class level, ability modifiers (magic items count) and the edition, never saved; a number the player types over a maximum is kept in `max` (↺ goes back). Spending is never blocked by the box (ticks can be set freely); the actions below refuse when nothing is left.
  - **Recharge** per row: short (a short or long rest gives them all back), long, short_one (2024: one back on a short rest, all on a long rest) or none (reset by hand: 2014 Divine Intervention's 7 days). `shortRest`/`longRest` (§6.8) call `restClassFeatures`.
  - **The rows** (PHB 2014 / PHB 2024 pages on each): Barbarian Rage (2014 2-6 and unlimited at 20, long rest; 2024 2-6, one back on a short rest; note with the rage damage), Relentless Rage, Persistent Rage (2024); Bard Bardic Inspiration (Cha mod, short rest from 5, the die by level); Cleric Channel Divinity (2014 1/2/3 short rest; 2024 2/3/4 one back), Divine Intervention; Druid Wild Shape (wild-shape.js's uses); Fighter Second Wind (2014 1, 2024 2/3/4), Action Surge, Indomitable; Monk Ki Points (2024 Focus Points, with the save DC), Martial Arts die, Uncanny Metabolism (2024); Paladin Divine Sense (2014), Lay on Hands (5 × level), Paladin's Smite and Faithful Steed (2024), Channel Divinity, Aura of Protection, Cleansing Touch (2014); Ranger (2024) Favored Enemy free Hunter's Mark casts, Tireless, Nature's Veil (2014 rangers have nothing to count); Rogue Sneak Attack, Stroke of Luck; Sorcerer Innate Sorcery (2024), Sorcery Points, Sorcerous Restoration (2024); Warlock Magical Cunning and Contact Patron (2024), Mystic Arcanum 6th-9th, Eldritch Master (2014); Wizard Arcane Recovery, Signature Spells; Artificer (Tasha's) Infusions, Flash of Genius.

  1. **Rules checks on the server** (free, instant, `sheetChecks`): no class; levels past 20; XP that doesn't match the level (only when XP is a number, so milestone groups aren't nagged); base ability scores above 20 or below 3; multiclass prerequisites (13 in each class's abilities); spells above the character's highest slot; expertise with no rogue or bard (or 9th-level ranger); and numbers typed over the automatic ones that the rules disagree with (proficiency bonus, spell and Pact Magic slots, saving throw proficiencies, maximum hit points above what the hit dice can give).
  2. **The AI** (task `review`, an agent run): it gets the rules parts of the sheet (`sheetForReview`: race, background, classes and subclasses, XP, scores, saves, skills, typed-over numbers, proficiencies, features, spells, gear with proficiency; never the backstory, looks or notes), the campaign's rules edition, the group's books and what the server already found, and checks class and subclass features for the level, racial traits, skill and spell counts, class spell lists, feats and ability increases, gear proficiency. It looks rules up in the books with Ask's book tools (`search_books`, `read_book`, `book_contents`), and only when they don't cover something (or there are no books and it isn't sure) uses `look_up_online` (a `research()` run, at most `SHEET_REVIEW_WEB_LOOKUPS`, 3). It ends by calling `submit_review` with a summary and findings, each with where the rule is (book and page, web address, or the AI's knowledge).
  - Each finding is a **problem** (against the rules as written) or **worth checking** (a feat, item, house rule or the DM may explain it). The page shows them above the sheet under "Against the rules" and "Worth checking", with the edition, until closed. The AI part counts towards `SHEET_AI_PER_HOUR`; when it can't run (the limit, the spending cap, an error) the rules checks still come back with why.
  - The automatic numbers on the sheet still follow the 2014 rules (§6.3); the AI judges the rest by the campaign's edition.
- **Limits:** `SHEET_AI_PER_HOUR` (60) AI calls per player for uploads, picture descriptions, non-SRD lookups and sheet reviews; 5 MB per save; 30 MB PDFs.
