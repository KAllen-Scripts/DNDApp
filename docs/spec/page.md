# The web page in detail

SPEC §3.3 has the outline; this is the detail of the frame every tab sits in.

- **Name:** the site is **The Party Ledger** (owner, 2026-10-21): the browser tab (with the campaign's name once in: "Test Campaign · The Party Ledger"; a popped-out page shows its tab and campaign) and the login page say so.
- **Top bar** (owner, 2026-10-21): one row at the usual desktop widths (checked at 1280, 1440 and 1920 px, DM and player): who you are, the tabs, then small icon-and-word buttons **Pop out**, **Dice** and (the DM) **Rest**, and an **account menu** (⚙ your name ▾) that opens under it with **Campaign settings** (the DM), **Look**, **Change password** and **Log out**; a click elsewhere, Escape, or choosing one closes it. Under 1180 px wide the three tools show only their icons (their names are in their tooltips). Every theme and layout keeps it to one row (feedback 2026-10-28: "Some themes mess with the layout"; themes with spaced capital tabs like 8-bit or Nine Hells ran onto two or three rows): the page tries the bar and, while it would run onto another row, steps down: the tools show only their icons, then the tabs close up, then the tabs go onto two lines in their own box between the campaign's name and the tools. The name keeps at least 8rem first. Not on a phone or with the app layout (tabs along the bottom). (`page/topbar-fit.js`.) On a phone the account menu's buttons are tiles in the ☰ sheet like the rest. (`index.html`, `app.js` `initAccountMenu`.)
- **Markdown descriptions** (owner, 2026-10-21): text that's often Markdown (tables, bold, lists) is shown formatted, through the same sanitised renderer as Ask's answers and stat blocks (`markdown.js`): an item's description in the DM's item dialog and Details, and at a merchant; a handout's text; and on the sheet an inventory item's description, a spell's description and "At higher levels", and Features & traits (and Additional features). Where it can be changed it's shown formatted until clicked or **Edit text** is pressed, which shows the Markdown to edit; clicking away or **Show formatted** formats it again; empty, it's just the text box (`markdownEditor`).
- **Look** (per browser, saved in localStorage, applied before first paint by `page/look-boot.js`): 12 themes (colours, system fonts, background art; Tavern follows the device's light/dark), 4 layouts (classic, sidebar, full width, app with bottom tabs), 3 text sizes, 4 chat styles (bubbles, play script, letters, terminal), 6 sheet styles (match theme, official, grimoire, index cards, blueprint, terminal) and 5 sheet layouts (three columns, combat first, by ability like the 2024 sheet, tabs like the sheet apps, one column). Also (2026-10-08, the owner asked for more layout options): an accent colour of your own over any theme; the tabs' order, which are hidden (never all) and which opens first; and for the map page, the toolbar's place (above, below, or down the left), what goes beside the map and on which side (§6.4 "On the map"; the side cards are greyed out and can't be picked while nothing is beside the map), token names (always, on hover, never) and which of the general map tools show. The dialog is grouped (Page, Ask, Sheet, Map). The bar along the top (campaign, tabs, Dice, Look…) is always the same width whichever tab is open (`--bar-width`: 1280px, 1180px in the sidebar layout, the whole window in full width), and every tab keeps room for its name in bold, so nothing in it moves when you switch tabs (owner, 2026-10-10). Only the panel under it changes width (`--page-width`: 860px for Ask, Notes, Handouts and the Archivist, 1280px for the sheet, Inventory, the map and the DM's lists). All in `themes.css`, keyed by `data-*` attributes; previews in the dialog reuse the same CSS. Only system fonts, nothing loaded from outside. Printing is always black on white.
- **Phone layout** (owner, 2026-10-14): `page/look-boot.js` marks `data-phone` on `<html>` on a phone (narrower than 640px, or a touch screen under 500px tall) or when Look → Page → **Phone layout** says Always (Automatic by default; Never turns it off). Then each page fills the screen (`100dvh`), the top bar is hidden, and a ☰ button in the top right corner opens it as a sheet from the bottom: the pages as tiles, then Dice, Rest, Campaign settings, Look, Change password, Log out; choosing anything, tapping outside or Escape closes it. A toolbar with more than fits folds into a **⋯** button that opens the rest as a list from the bottom (the same buttons, only shown differently; `phone.js`). Nothing goes beside the map on a phone, and tabs don't focus their text box there (the keyboard would cover the page). Styles in `mobile.css`, every rule scoped to `:root[data-phone]`, loaded after `themes.css` so it overrides the layouts. Built page by page: Ask (the chat's buttons in ⋯, a one-line question box that grows), Notes, Handouts, the Sheet (its bar sticks to the top with Next d20 and the save status; Review, Upload, Download, Print and Pop out in ⋯) the Inventory (item box and Add in one row, Add by hand in ⋯) and the Map (the map fills the screen; Fit, Measure, Ping, Draw and Initiative float in a row at the bottom and ⋯ opens every tool as a sheet; the DM's fog and wall tools are strips that scroll sideways; no Beside the map, Sheet window or Full screen on a phone, and the toolbar place under Look doesn't apply), the dice tray and every dialog but the map creator (sheets from the bottom, full width, their buttons kept at the bottom; the tray doesn't focus its text box) and the DM's Creatures, Items and Merchants (Find and + New in a row at the top, Find online and Look up in ⋯), the login screens (the form fills the screen) and the admin screen (Look, Change password and Log out in ⋯); the plan is `docs/notes/mobile-ui.md`.
