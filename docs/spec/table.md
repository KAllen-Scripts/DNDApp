# Handouts, rests, campaign settings (SPEC §6.7, §6.8, §6.10)

## 6.7 Handouts

Owner's request (2026-10-08): the DM gives players a picture and/or text (a letter, a wanted poster, a riddle, a map scrap), to everyone or to chosen players.

- **Page** (Handouts tab, `web/public/campaign/table.js`): the DM gets a form (title, text, picture, Everyone or ticked players) and, on each handout, who got it, "Change who gets it" and "Take back". Players see the handouts given to them, newest first (the text formatted as Markdown, §3.3); a new one arrives live (on the campaign's live stream) and puts a count on the tab until it's opened. A picture is shown smaller; a click shows it full width.
- **Server** (`src/table/handouts.js`): `to` is "everyone" (everyone in the campaign, including people who join later) or user ids, all checked to be in the campaign. Players never see handouts not given to them, their pictures, or who else got one. Taking one back hides it from everyone (players' pages drop it live); the archive keeps it.
- **Archive:** the picture as uploaded and `handouts/<id>/changes.jsonl` (the whole handout after each change); restore takes the last line.
- **Archivist:** new, changed and taken-back handouts go to the archivist between sessions (§5.2) with who got them: their text, and what the AI read in the picture (writing word for word, then what it shows; owner's request, 2026-10-10). The archivist treats both as known only by the players who got it. The reading isn't stored; a rebuild reads the pictures again.

## 6.8 Rests and hit dice

Owner's request (2026-10-09): "a long rest and short rest feature. And a way to spend hit die." Also needed for merchants restocking "every x number of long rests". Defaults picked by Claude: 5e rules for the group's edition; the DM calls long rests for the party; players take short rests and spend hit dice themselves, rolled through the dice roller.

- **Rules** (`shared/sheet.js`: `hitDicePool`, `hitDice`, `spendHitDie`, `shortRest`, `longRest`, all pure, the server applies them):
  - **Hit dice** are kept per die size: `hit_dice_spent` `{ "10": 2, "6": 1 }` (replaces the single `hit_dice_used` number; an old sheet's number is counted from the biggest die down). The pool is read from the Hit dice box, so a typed value ("5d8") counts. Sizes d4 to d12 and d20.
  - **Spending one:** roll the die plus the Constitution modifier; regain that many hit points (never fewer than none), up to the maximum. A blank Current hit points counts as full.
  - **Short rest:** Pact Magic slots come back, and class features that come back on a short rest (§6.4: all of them, or one for the 2024 rows that say so, like Rage, Second Wind and Wild Shape); the player then spends hit dice.
  - **Long rest:** all hit points back, temporary hit points gone, every spell slot, Pact Magic slot and class feature use back (except rows reset by hand), death saves cleared, spent hit dice back: up to half the character's total (at least one), the biggest first, under the **2014** rules; all of them under **2024**. Under 2014 a character at 0 hit points when it starts gets nothing from it. Exhaustion isn't on the sheet, so it isn't touched.
  - **Edition:** the campaign's rules edition (§6.10, `editions.js`): the DM's setting, else `REST_RULES` (2014 or 2024), else the group's Player's Handbook in the books folder (§5.3) when it is exactly one edition, else 2014 (the sheet's own rules are 2014).
- **Players** (Hit dice box on the sheet): a row per die size with ticks (editable by hand, like the slots) and how many are left; **Spend** calls `POST /sheet/hit-dice`, where the server rolls through the roll log (`rolls.js`, so it's shared by the tray's "Who sees my rolls" and the dice animate), applies it and saves the sheet with the reason; **Short rest** calls `POST /sheet/short-rest`. Unsaved typing is saved first. The DM has no sheet, so no hit dice.
- **The DM** (Rest button in the header, `web/public/campaign/rests.js`): ticks for each player (all by default), Short rest and Long rest, which rules apply, and the last rests. `POST /rests` changes every sheet in it (players with no sheet yet are still counted), each saved with the reason ("long rest (called by the DM, 2014 rules)") and `by` the DM, so the archive and the archivist see why.
- **Live:** the rest goes out on the campaign live stream (`rest`) to the players in it and the DM. A player's page shows a note and reloads the sheet (with unsaved changes, saving them meets the newer version and the usual conflict question applies); other windows with the sheet follow (BroadcastChannel).
- **Kept and counted (the hook for other features):** each rest the DM calls is source data, archive first (`rests.jsonl`, one line each: `{ id, kind, at, by, edition, user_ids, skipped }`), mirrored in `rests` (schema v13) and restored. In the server, `rests.events` emits `'rest'` with the rest and its `campaign_id` after the sheets are saved, and `rests.count(cid, { kind: 'long', since })` says how many long rests have been called after a time, so something that happens "every N long rests" (a merchant restocking) can keep the time it last happened and count, or react to the event. Players' own short rests aren't in the log.
- **Not changed by rests:** NPC and enemy tokens' hit points, item charges (owner, 2026-10-10), and anything the archivist knows. Player character tokens show the sheet's hit points, so they follow (§6.6).
## 6.10 Campaign settings

Owner (2026-10-09): "the campaign setting should have an option to ignore weight limits."

- The DM's **Campaign settings** in the top bar's account menu (players don't have it) opens Campaign settings. It holds two settings. **Rules edition** (owner, 2026-10-09; built 2026-10-10): 2014 rules, 2024 rules, or not chosen (the default: `REST_RULES`, else the edition of the group's Player's Handbook in the books folder, else 2014; the dialog says which that is now). Rests (§6.8), Ask's rules answers (§5.3) and lookups in the books (spells, stat blocks, Find online, items: that edition's books first, then any) follow it, through `editions.js`. The character sheet's own rules stay 2014 whatever it says (later, §6.3). **Weight limits**: carrying capacity (default), variant encumbrance, or ignore weight limits (§6.4). **Attacks from the map** (2026-10-22, §6.6): on or off (on by default), whether damage from players' attacks waits for the DM's OK, and whether players see the AC they rolled against (`attacks: { on, damage: auto | dm, show_ac }`, `normalizeAttackSettings`). **Levelling up** (2026-10-28, §6.4): who starts a level up, `level_up: player | dm` (players when they're ready, the default; or only once the DM gives them a level), and the party's levels with Give a level, Take back and Give everyone a level.
- **Your own backgrounds** (`backgrounds`, `shared/src/backgrounds.js` `normalizeCustomBackgrounds`; up to 50): name, skills, tools, languages, equipment, gold, a feature (2014-style) and/or three abilities and an origin feat (2024-style). Made, edited and removed in Campaign settings; every player's sheet suggests them live (§6.4 Backgrounds). Removing one leaves sheets that took it as they are.
- Kept per campaign (`campaign_settings`, `shared/src/settings.js` `normalizeSettings`), archived as `settings.json` with history, restored on start-up. `PATCH /campaigns/:cid/settings` (DM); everyone gets them with their campaigns (`GET /me`, `GET /campaigns/:cid`) and hears changes live (`settings` on the campaign's live stream), so sheets work themselves out again at once. The server uses them wherever it works a sheet out (token speed on maps, the DM's equipped view, the archivist's sheet text).
