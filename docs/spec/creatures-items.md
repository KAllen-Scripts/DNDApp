# The DM's creatures, items and merchants (SPEC §6.6, §6.9)

- **The DM's creatures** (owner's request, 2026-10-08): the DM keeps a library of enemies and friendly NPCs instead of a character sheet (the Creatures tab, called the **Bestiary** since 2026-10-28, replaces the Sheet tab for the DM; the DM has no Sheet window, the page never loads a sheet for them, the server refuses sheet requests from the `dm` role, and any DM sheet saved before this is ignored by the archivist; players are unchanged). Each has a name, kind (enemy or NPC), size, colour, max hit points, AC, speed, darkvision, a stat block (typed, or filled by the AI from the name as for tokens; the AI also sets hit points and size unless set), a picture, and notes only the DM sees (`src/creatures/store.js`, `web/public/creatures/creatures.js`). In the Edit dialog a saved stat block is shown formatted; "Edit text" swaps in its Markdown to change it, "Show stat block" swaps back. "Place on map" (or picking it under "Who is it?" in Add token) asks how many (up to 20) and whether hidden; they appear in a row in the middle of the view, numbered when there are several ("Goblin 1".."Goblin 4", carrying on from tokens already named so), each with the creature's hit points, stat block, darkvision, speed, record link and picture. The picture is copied once into that map's token pictures, so a map stays whole on its own. A placed token is ordinary from then on: changing or removing the creature doesn't touch it. "Save to creatures" on a selected NPC or enemy token keeps it (picture and stat block too; "Goblin 3" is saved as "Goblin"). Archived like handouts; the archivist doesn't read them (they're preparation; what players meet reaches it through map events).
- **Bestiary and People** (owner's feedback, 2026-10-28): each saved creature has a `group`: `creature` (a kind of creature: Goblin, Giant Shore Crab) or `person` (one person: Mira the innkeeper, the villain). The tab (still `creatures` in code and the API) has a **Bestiary | People** switch with counts (remembered per browser). The bestiary puts each under its **type** (the DM's own `type`, else the stat block's size line: "*Medium undead, neutral evil*" → undead; the 14 5e types in book order, "No type yet" last) as headings, weakest challenge first, with a chip per type to show one. People are listed by name with their **role** ("Runs the Gilded Goose"). `kind` stays friendly (`npc`) or hostile (`enemy`), apart from the group, so a villain is a hostile person and a guard dog a friendly creature. One saved before the split is a person when friendly, else a creature (`shared/src/bestiary.js` `groupOf`); Edit's "Goes in" moves one across. A card has **Wiki page** when the DM's wiki has a page of that title or other name (opens in the drawer). Find online in People files the result there. Ask lists each as "person, friendly" or "creature (undead), hostile" (`PIPELINE_VERSION` 39).
- **Find a creature online** (owner's request, 2026-10-08): "Find online" on the Creatures tab. The DM says what they want ("Ember Wyrmling", "a crystal golem", "the Hollow King from Grimhollow"); if the group's books print the creature (as for stat blocks above), its stat block comes from the book (`source: 'book'`, with the book and page) and the AI searches the web only for a picture (purpose `creature:find-book`); otherwise the AI (task `maps`, purpose `creature:find`) searches the web, official sources or not (homebrew sites, wikis, forums; another edition is converted), reads the page, and writes the 5e stat block, the page it came from and picture addresses; a second call (`creature:tidy`) turns that into fields. The server downloads the first picture that is really a picture (`net/fetch-public.js`: http(s) on ports 80/443 only, every connection and redirect checked after the name lookup so nothing on this machine or the home network is reached, 10 MB, 15 s) and keeps it like an uploaded one. It runs in the background: the creature is listed at once as searching (`finding: { query, status: 'pending' }`), the page checks every 3 s, and it fills in (stat block `source: 'web'`, `source: { url, title, official, picture }` shown as a link) or fails with a reason ("Search again"). A search cut short by a restart is marked failed on the next start. It can't be placed while searching. Counts against `MAP_AI_PER_HOUR`. With `LLM_PROVIDER=claude-code`, Claude Code's WebSearch and WebFetch are the only tools allowed for it; with `api`, Anthropic's `web_search_20260209` and `web_fetch_20260209` (Opus/Sonnet 4.6 or later).
- **Later, creatures** (owner's request, 2026-10-08): once the group's books are all scanned in, a ready-made creature library to choose from (built from the books' stat blocks).
## 6.9 Merchants and items

Owner's request (2026-10-09): "merchant tokens… the DM can set up and add items to. The items list will work similar to the creatures tab… Merchants can then have these items, with a stock level and price, that players can buy on their own, without having to go through the DM… restock every x number of long rests."

- **Items** (the DM's Items, a view of the wiki's space, `src/items/store.js`, `web/public/items/items.js`): kept once, like creatures. Each has a name, kind (weapon, armour, adventuring gear, tool, potion, scroll, magic item, other), the numbers for its kind (below), rarity, attunement, a usual price (stored in copper pieces), weight, a description players see at a merchant (Markdown), notes only the DM sees, and a picture. Three ways to get one besides typing it: **Look up** by name looks in the DM's own items first (no AI call), then the group's books (keyword search for the name, then the AI copies the entry from that page and the next, labelled with the book and page; purpose `item:book`), then the AI's knowledge of 5e (`item:ai`, labelled as the AI's). The same order as creatures' stat blocks. **Fill in** does the same for one already saved (a price the DM set stays). **Find online** is like creatures': a web research run (`item:find`) and a tidy-up call (`item:tidy`), in the background, with a picture from the public internet only; `source` keeps the page, its title and whether it's official. Prices the AI gives ("50 gp") are read into copper (`parsePrice` in `shared/src/coins.js`). Removing an item takes it off the list; merchants that stock it keep selling it. **Numbers for its kind** (owner, 2026-10-16): the item form shows the fields of the kind chosen, under "what it does on a sheet": a weapon's damage, Strength / Dexterity / Finesse, simple or martial, properties and magic bonus; armour's type (light, medium, heavy, shield), base AC (a shield's AC bonus), Strength needed, Stealth disadvantage and magic bonus; what a potion heals; a magic item's charges (and what brings them back) and its effects on a sheet (the inventory's list: AC, saves, scores, speed…). Other kinds have none. They start from what the app reads (the PHB tables by name, else the description, `itemStats`), and are saved as the item's own (`stats`, `normalizeItemStats` in `shared/src/gear.js`, only the parts of its kind) once the DM changes one; until then the item keeps being read from its description. A buyer's inventory line gets them as they are (`addToInventory`), so equipping a bought armour sets the AC with no reading of the text. The item's card shows them ("AC 15 · heavy · +1 (set by you)"). Fill in clears them (a new description is read again). The player can still change any line, and a typed AC on the sheet still wins (the DM may overrule the rules). **Pictures** (owner, 2026-10-10): the DM uploads one (New item, or Picture on a card), Find online brings one, and Look up and Fill in bring none. The picture shows in Items, in a merchant's shop, in the Inventory of a player who bought it (`GET /campaigns/:cid/gear/pictures`, `GET /campaigns/:cid/gear/items/:iid/picture`: only items in the asker's own inventory, kept even after the DM takes the item off the list) and in the DM's equipped list. Lines a player adds themselves have no picture. Below the list, **What the players have equipped** shows each player's character, AC (marked when they typed it themselves) and equipped items with to hit and damage, not proficient, attuned (`GET /campaigns/:cid/gear/equipped`; loaded with the tab, and Refresh), so the DM can check for cheating (§6.4). Equipped items that are the DM's own (bought from a merchant) show their picture there.
- **Merchants** (the DM's Merchants, a view of the wiki's space, `src/items/merchants.js`, `web/public/items/merchants.js`): a name, a description players see, notes only the DM sees, a token colour (gold by default), a picture, open or closed, and **stock**: lines of an item, its price here (the item's usual price unless the DM sets one), how many are left (or no limit) and a **restock level** (by default what it started with; empty: restocking leaves it alone). "Add item" takes any name: one not in Items yet is looked up and added there. Its **Make a new item** button (owner, 2026-10-10) opens the same form as Items' New item (name, kind, rarity, attunement, usual price, weight, description, notes, picture) plus In stock and Restock to: the item is saved to Items (as the DM's own) and sold here at its usual price. The DM changes a line's price and levels, stops selling it, restocks by hand ("Restock now"), sees what players see ("See shop"), and sees the last 50 sales (who, what, how many, for how much).
- **On the map:** "Place on map" puts a token in the middle of the view: an NPC token with the merchant's name, colour and picture, and `merchant: <id>` (players can't change it). It has a gold ⚖ badge. Clicking it (anyone who can see it) shows a **Shop** button. Closing the merchant down leaves its tokens as ordinary NPCs.
- **Buying** (players, `POST /campaigns/:cid/merchants/:mid/buy` `{ line, qty }`): the shop shows what each costs, how many are left and the coins on their sheet. The page asks once, saves anything waiting on the sheet, and the server then, with no waiting in between (so two buyers can't both take the last one): checks the shop is open and has enough; takes the price from the sheet's coins, big coins first, breaking a coin and giving change in gold, silver and copper if needed (`payCoins`; refused, saying what it costs and what they have, if they can't afford it); adds the item to the sheet's inventory (§6.4: a new line with its weapon or armour stats and Proficient from the class, or more of a line already there for that item or name; `addToInventory` in `shared/src/gear.js`); saves the sheet as a new version with the reason ("bought 2 × Potion of Healing from Mira for 100 gp"), so it reaches the archivist like any sheet change; and lowers the stock. The sheet on screen (and in a popped-out window) shows the new version. The DM can't buy (no sheet). Not built: selling items back.
- **Restocking:** a merchant can restock every N long rests (`restock.every`). Each line with a restock level and fewer left goes back up to it (never down). Each long rest the DM calls (§6.8; one call is one party rest) counts: `context.js` listens to `rests.events` and calls `merchants.longRest`, which keeps the count and the last rest and restock times on the merchant (archived with it). Short rests don't count, and players can't long-rest on their own.
- **Live:** any change to a merchant (a sale, a restock, the DM's edits) sends `merchant {id}` on the campaign's live stream; an open shop and the DM's list look again.
- **Archive:** like creatures: `items/<id>/` and `merchants/<id>/`, the whole entry after each change, pictures as uploaded; restored on start-up. The archivist doesn't read them; purchases reach it through the sheet changes.
- **Later:** selling items to merchants; haggling or prices per player; the archivist hearing about sales directly.
